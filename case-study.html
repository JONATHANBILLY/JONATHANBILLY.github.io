Featured Case Study

Olist E-Commerce Sales & Retention Analysis

Power BI · DAX · Star Schema · 11 Tables

01 · Business Problem

Analyze ~100K orders across 11 relational tables (Jan–Aug 2018 vs. Jan–Aug 2017) to uncover regional revenue concentration, payment behavior, and retention drivers.

02 · Modeling & DAX

Engineered a clean star schema semantic model with custom DAX measures for YoY growth, running totals, repeat customer rate, regional share, and delivery lead time.

03 · Strategic Outcome

Identified high single-purchase churn (97%) and ~3× AOV uplift from multi-installment credit payments, translating raw metrics into actionable bundling & regional promo levers.

Key Findings & Business Recommendations

40%

Geographic concentration requires localized marketing. São Paulo holds 40% of total revenue and the top 3 states hold 64%. While Rio de Janeiro clusters in a single metro area (54%), Minas Gerais spreads across 600+ cities—calling for distinct logistics and acquisition playbooks.

~3×

Credit card installments strongly drive Average Order Value. Orders paid in 6+ installments average R$317 compared to R$98 for single payments, making installment promotion a primary lever for higher-ticket categories.

97%

High one-time buyer rate shifts focus to first-order monetization. Because repeat purchases are rare, revenue growth depends on maximizing initial basket size through product bundling (2+ item orders average R$250 vs. R$150) and free-shipping thresholds.
